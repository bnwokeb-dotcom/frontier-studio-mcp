## 2. Creature Realism: Nonhuman Jurisprudence Without "Rubber Monster" Tropes

Audiences praised `THE LOST KIND` and `NIBIRU` for believable creatures and mocked sluggish cliché monsters:
1. **Functional Anatomy Over Spikes:** Asymmetric dorsal scarring, overlapping weathered osteoderms that part *around* bronzed collar clasps, throat gill-slits that subtly inflate `0.5s` *before* vocalizing.
2. **Ocular Anchor Rule:** Never use dead glowing LED eyes. Specify *deeply recessed convex corneal lenses reflecting the overcast sky/torches, moist sclera capillaries, and horizontal or multi-lobed slit pupils*.
3. **Subsurface & Moisture Discipline:** Localized oil sheen in joint crevices and wet membranes backlit with warm red subsurface scattering, contrasted against dry dusty plates on shoulders/knuckles.
4. **Weight & Voice Contrast:** Heavy asynchronous hip-shoulder shift displacing dust/gravel. **Zero cartoon roars** — voice is calm, unhurried, administrative baritone layered with dry sub-tracheal click foley (`48kHz` mono enrolled stem).

## 3. Anti-Morphing Combat & Crowd Grammar ("Edited, Never One Prompt")

To eliminate every AI slapstick complaint spotted in YouTube comments (`soldier cut himself`, `enemy drops without hit`, `fake slow-mo`):
1. **One Physical Mechanical Action Per Clip (`3–5s` bursts, real-time 24fps speed):**
   - Clip A (`24mm Wide`): Spatial positions & blockade distance.
   - Clip B (`50mm Medium`): Specific hand/weapon latch or parry trajectory (`A -> B` clear background).
   - Clip C (`85mm Close`): Exact contact impact + immediate displacement of cloth/dust/sparks.
   - Clip D (`50mm Reaction`): Victim/observer involuntary shock recoil.
2. **Never Prompt Slow-Motion Brawls:** Slow motion amplifies neural interpolation smearing. Keep action crisp real-time; reserve stillness (`0.8s` held eye reaction) for psychological shock.

## 4. High-CTR Dark Cinema Thumbnail Engineering (The 150px Mobile Test)

Over 70% of clicks happen on 120–160px phone thumbnails where dark moody stills turn into black boxes:
1. **Dedicated `16:9` Thumbnail Shoot (Never Grab a Dark `2.39:1` Frame):** Generate a bespoke `16:9` `flow_prompt_plate` / `keyframe_build` composition.
2. **Minimum 50% Figure-Ground Luminance Delta:** Place the dark armored creature & kneeling human against **pale overcast courtyard mist / chalk sandstone horizon** OR slice a bright diagonal skylight shaft behind their silhouettes.
3. **Single Color Magnet + Eye Punch:** Keep armor/stone muted obsidian & slate; place high saturation ONLY on the narrative contradiction (a bright oxidized bronze shackle snapping onto the king's crimson/gold sleeve + sharp specular catchlights inside eyes).
4. **Zero Collage / Zero Arrows / Bottom-Right Safe:** Timestamp overlays cover the bottom-right corner — keep all story action in the left 75% and upper 80%.
