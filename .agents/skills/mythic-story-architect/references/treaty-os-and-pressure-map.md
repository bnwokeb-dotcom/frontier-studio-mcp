# Treaty Operating System & 18-Minute Pressure Map Reference

## 1. World Operating System (Replace Fantasy Lore Dumps With Enforceable Rules)
Every Dark Mythological Procedural Thriller defines these 8 operational vectors *before* Scene 1:

| Vector | Definition Rule | Exemplar (*The Black Accord*) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Institution** | Named governing court, guild, or priesthood with records and jurisdiction. | Crown Treasury vs. Subterranean Ossari Judiciary. |
| **Treaty** | Specific bilateral dependence neither side can afford to break openly. | 500-year Accord: Ossari anti-fungal crops for crown mineral quotas. |
| **Enforcement** | Unstoppable automatic penalty triggered by physical evidence/seal. | Royal override of custody transfers personal jurisdiction to Ossari court. |
| **Biology** | Concrete nonhuman anatomy, metabolism, limit, or reproductive cycle. | Incubation vaults sensitive to iron vibration & daylight UV. |
| **Resource** | What finite asset both sides fight over physically. | Deep limestone incubation seams beneath old quarries. |
| **Jurisdiction** | Physical threshold lines (`boundary stones`, `chalk seams`) visibly demarcated. | Sandstone execution dais vs. iron-pinned descent gate. |
| **Leverage** | Documented paradox allowing the protagonist to weaponize enemy rules. | Joint-liability audit clause strips royal immunity when auditor surrenders seal. |
| **Irreversible Price** | What victory permanently costs the protagonist (`mind`, `body`, `freedom`). | Auditor Mara forfeits her freedom alongside the king inside permanent Ossari detention. |

## 2. The 18-Minute Narrative Pressure Map (`~216` Shots)
1. **`0:00–0:30` Cold Open:** Impossible procedural encounter + violation + arrest/seal trigger.
2. **`0:30–2:00` Physical Geography:** Establish locked crucible boundaries (`24–35mm` wide/medium).
3. **`2:00–4:00` Rule #1 Demonstrated:** Show why breaking the smallest institutional protocol has lethal physical weight.
4. **`4:00–6:00` Deliver Title Promise:** Fulfill the contradiction promised in the YouTube title cleanly—then pivot immediately (`BUT`).
5. **`6:00–9:00` Hidden Institutional Conflict:** Reveal both factions have dirty stakes; protagonist's `Actual Objective` surfaces.
6. **`9:00–11:00` Midpoint Inversion:** Intelligent opponent counters the evidence and flips the lead's **objective** (not merely escalating brute danger).
7. **`11:00–13:00` Constrained Operation:** Protagonist operates physically without previous shelter/tools under tight enemy surveillance.
8. **`13:00–15:00` Unavoidable Biological/Moral Price:** Paying the irreversible cost becomes the only legal weapon left.
9. **`15:00–17:00` Recontextualizing Leverage:** Earlier planted prop/signature (`0:04` seal) snaps shut around the antagonist.
10. **`17:00–18:00` Aftermath & Larger World:** Concrete proof of survival inside a bigger continent/season arc.
