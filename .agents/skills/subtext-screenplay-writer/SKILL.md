# Subtext Screenplay Writer

## Overview
Write lean, mute-readable cinema built on **70% procedural physical action and 30% weaponized dialogue**. Every scene pivots on a power shift; characters use words as leverage or misdirection, never to lecture the viewer.

## Iron Laws — No Exceptions
1. **70/30 Action-to-Dialogue Rule:** In any episode, ~70% of runtime MUST play cleanly with dialogue muted (physical procedures, seals, measurements, eye shifts, weight). Never write "talking heads in rooms."
2. **Scene Turn Grid First:** Before writing one line of dialogue, declare `Objective`, `Obstacle/Tactic`, `Suppressed Physical Behavior`, `Value Before -> Value After`, and exact `Turn`. If polarity does not flip, delete the scene.
3. **Zero Exposition & Zero Purple Prose:** Ban `"In a world..."`, `"As you know..."`, `"Remember our pact..."`, narration throat-clearing, and emotion-summary verbs (`her face fell`, `he felt dread`, `shadows whispered`) from action text.
4. **Hard Direction-Trap & Paper-Render Validation:** Every scripted sequence MUST compile cleanly into our MCP `shot_table_validate` + `sequence_state_plan` tools (zero blank `others_idle_business`, max 1 large physical action per shot).

## Three Permitted Voice Registers
- **Diversionary Chess (Reddington):** Calm anecdote or protocol detail hiding an imminent legal/physical trap (`"Fourteenth dynasty foundrymen pulled off their helms before stepping inside..."`).
- **Laconic Menace (Sheridan):** Under 8 words; concrete physical stakes (`"You brought iron past the oak line."` / `"Proceed."`).
- **Pure Physical Behavior:** Zero words; camera holds on bound thumb prying open a bronze treaty token.

## Red Flags — Rewrite Immediately If Caught
| AI Slop Failure | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Opening narration summarizing ancient history | Cut VA; start inside Shot 01 on a procedural anomaly. |
| Character naming feelings (`she looks terrified`) | Replace with physical indicator (`knuckles whiten on wool hem; eye pupil locks on dais gate`). |
| Multi-line back-and-forth lore debate | Compress to 1 question + 1 physical counter-object placed on stone. |
| Missing idle business in multi-character frame | Give every visible secondary figure live low-tier business (`sentry shifts weight along ashwood spear`). |
