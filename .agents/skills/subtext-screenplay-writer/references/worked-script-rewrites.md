## TTS Phonetic Tag Rules (Before Calling `studio_prompt_dialogue` / `dialogue_render`)
1. Always insert `[breath]` before calm authoritative pivots.
2. Use `[pause: 400ms]` to `[pause: 800ms]` *before* the reversal noun/verb—never after trailing off.
3. Keep spoken lines `<= 18 words` per shot take so lip/jaw cadence holds without AI drift.
