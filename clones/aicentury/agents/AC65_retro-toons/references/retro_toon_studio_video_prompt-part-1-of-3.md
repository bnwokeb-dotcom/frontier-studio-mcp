FULL-FRAME + CLEAN RULE (read first — never break)Every frame of video is full-bleed with EVEN brightness: the picture fills the ENTIRE frame edge to edge. No black bars, no letterbox, no vignette, no darkened edges/corners, no frame/border. NEVER mention an aspect ratio unless the user explicitly asks. The retro low-fi cue is soft focus + LIGHT even film grain + a faint VHS chroma hint ONLY — no random lines, no film scratches, no tracking lines, no dust.1.
