# Edit plan: cutting the reel together

This file governs the edit plan: how the eight 2-second clips become one finished reel.

> From the user's Rotterdam reel (2026-11-11): 8 clips, 11.3 seconds, 24 frames a second, one soft finish over everything, a pixel caption on shot 1 only, the trend's sound. Any editor works, CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci, or a script.

---

## The cut list

Cut each clip to the trend's own rhythm, so the cuts land on the sound. These are the cut points of the Rotterdam reel, measured from the final file:

**Best:** open the trend video the user is copying in the editor, put their clips over it shot for shot, and cut where the original cuts. The table is the fallback. Take each clip's best stretch, usually from the start, before any drift.

Hard cuts only, no transitions, no zooms, no speed ramps.

**Only one or a few clips so far?** Post a single clip with the caption on it, or wait for all eight, the trend's punch is the quick cuts.

---

## One finish over everything

Every clip gets the same light finish, so shots from different times of day look like one game:

- **slightly softer** (a touch of blur or reduced sharpness)
- **less contrast**
- **slightly warmer**

Keep it subtle, the clips already carry the look; the finish only glues them together. Apply it as one adjustment layer over the whole timeline, not per clip.

---

## The caption

- **Shot 1 only**, then gone at the first cut.
- Small **pixel font**, white, lowercase, centred in the upper third, with a trailing ellipsis or colon: `how [subject] must feel like...` (a city, a game, a film, anything)
- In English by default (`how rotterdam must feel like...`); another language only when the user asks for it.

---

## The sound

Use the trend's own sound, on TikTok or Instagram, tap the sound on the trend post and use it, set so its beat falls on the cuts. Rotterdam used `i could be the one (Ambient) (sped up)` from 17.05 seconds into the track; the cut table above fits only that track at that start point. With another sound, cut on the trend video instead of the table. The clips themselves are silent.

---

## Export

Upright full HD (1080 × 1920), 24 frames a second. The clips come out at 30; the editor conforms them.

---

## Self-check for an edit plan

1. Eight cuts in the table (or the user's shot count), lengths adding up to the reel length.
2. One finish for the whole timeline; caption on shot 1 only.
3. The sound advice points at the trend they copied, with Rotterdam's track as the example.
