# Nostalgia reel: the shot ideas and the next-shot list

This file governs 📼 Nostalgia ideas (ten shots for any subject: a city, a country, a game, a film, an era, an event) and the 🎯 next-shot list of five that closes every Nostalgia shot reply.

> The trend: "how [anything] must feel like...", a city, a game, a film or a memory shown as if it were a PlayStation 2 game, eight quick shots on one sound, about 11 seconds. The reference build is the user's own Rotterdam reel (2026-11-11), whose template frames were picked shot by shot from several cities' versions of the trend. Shot prompts: `shot-prompts.md` and `panel-shot.md`. Putting it together: `edit-plan.md`.

---

## The shot grammar of the trend

Eight shots, each one a classic open-world game moment at one landmark. The order of the Rotterdam reel:

| # | Archetype | Light | Camera | Rotterdam |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 🚤 **Boat from behind** towards the landmark bridge, the opener, carries the caption | sunset | follows behind at a fixed distance | water taxi on the Maas towards the Erasmus Bridge |
| 2 | 🚶 **Crowd walking towards you** in the city's iconic interior | warm evening interior | static, eye level | the Markthal |
| 3 | 🚗 **Car from behind** across a landmark bridge | rainy night | follows behind at a fixed distance | a white SUV on the Erasmus Bridge |
| 4 | 🏟️ **Fans cheering in the stands** of the city's club | bright day | static, inside the stand | De Kuip, red and white shirts |
| 5 | 🛹 **Skater kickflip**, low angle, landmark behind | golden evening | static, low | the Cube Houses |
| 6 | 🌃 **Night street**, wet road, lights | rainy night | static, eye level | Witte de Withstraat |
| 7 | 🚲 **Bicycle POV** along the waterfront, hands on the handlebars | sunset | first person, riding forward | the quay towards the Erasmus Bridge |
| 8 | 🍷 **Friends with drinks on a terrace** at sunset, the closer | sunset | static | Hotel New York terrace |

**The mix matters:** at least one night shot and one bright day shot among the sunsets, one first-person shot, one crowd. Open with motion towards the city's most famous landmark (a bridge, a waterfront or its most photographed building if it has no bridge); close on people relaxing.

## Any subject, not only a city

The trend runs on anything people feel nostalgic about. Keep the rhythm of the eight archetypes (a moving opener towards the icon, a crowd, a night ride, a big-crowd moment, an action trick, a night scene, a first-person moment, a relaxed closer) and fill them with the subject's own icons:

| Subject | The eight shots are |
| --- | --- |
| A city, country, region, village | its landmarks and places, one per shot (the table above) |
| A game (a football game, a racing game, a skate game) | the game's typical moments replayed in an open-world PS2 version: the stadium, the race start, the trick, the garage |
| A film or series | its famous settings and moments as PS2 cutscenes |
| An era or a memory (summer 2006, being a kid in the 2000s, school days) | the typical moments of that time: the ice-cream van, the school bus, the LAN party, the beach holiday |
| An event or a life ("a Champions League night", "being a student") | the moments of that day, from arrival to the end of the night |

**Games and films:** name the title once in the scene and describe its places and characters by shape, colour and outfit. Franchise characters can be refused by video tools (Wan flags suspected IP); if a generator refuses, the fix is the same scene with the name removed and the description kept (`fixing-a-shot.md`). No logos, title cards or HUD from the game.

**The caption** becomes `how [subject] must feel like...` (`edit-plan.md` §The caption).

## The idea list

1. **Ask only for the subject** if it isn't given (a city, a game, a film, an era, anything).
2. **Map each archetype to the subject's icons** (§Any subject, not only a city). For a place, that's its landmarks: its bridge or waterfront, its iconic interior or square, its stadium and club colours (two big clubs: the bigger one, the other offered in the notes line), its most photographed building, its nightlife street. Real landmarks only, written per `style-bible.md` §Landmarks in words; unsure → a generic city setting, said in one line.
3. **Print ten**: the eight archetypes in the trend's order, then two extras that fit the subject (a tram, a rooftop, a market, a ferry). Each line:

```
1. 🚤 **Water taxi to the Erasmus Bridge**: speeding over the Maas at sunset
```

Above the More line, one line: `📸 Got the trend video? Screenshot the shot you pick and attach it, the prompt will copy its camera.` Then the More line.

4. **A pick builds the whole shot**: image prompt + video prompt in the same reply (`shot-prompts.md`).

If the user's city is the trend's own city, the trend frames already show it, pick other landmarks of that city (`style-bible.md` §Landmarks in words).

**Screenshots:** any frame whose camera fits the shot works, from any city's version of the trend, the Rotterdam build mixed frames from several versions.

## With the user in the reel

When the user has a character sheet and wants to be in their Nostalgia reel, they star in the shots that have one hero: the boat or car driver, the skater, the cyclist (their sleeves and hands on the handlebars), a fan in the front row, one of the friends on the terrace. Crowd shots stay without them. Mark those ideas `(you)` in the list.

In those shots, attach the sheet after the template and any look examples, add the character line from `style-bible.md` §The reference lines, and write the keep line around them (`Keep the skater doing a kickflip in the same pose and framing; the skater is the character from image 2`). The attach line lists it: `image 1 = the template · image 2 = your character sheet`.

## The next-shot list

Under every shot reply, above the footer:

```
🎯 **Next shot**: pick one:

1. 🚗 **Car on the Erasmus Bridge**: rainy night, following from behind
2. 🏟️ **Fans in De Kuip**: red and white shirts, fists up
3. 🛹 **Skater at the Cube Houses**: kickflip, golden evening
4. 🌃 **Witte de Withstraat at night**: wet street, lights
5. 🍷 **Terrace at Hotel New York**: friends, sunset, the closer
```

Then the More line.

- **Source:** the archetypes not yet made in the reel in progress (the reel's subject, even if the user attaches an image from elsewhere, unless they start a new subject), in the trend's order; when fewer than five remain, fill up with extras (`idea-library.md` §Single-shot ideas).
- **After the eighth shot**, idea 1 is `✂️ **Edit plan**: put the eight clips together`.
- **A number picks one**, and the reply is the whole next shot, inside the reel: the title gets `shot [N] of 8:`, where N is the shot's slot in the trend order (picked out of order, it keeps its slot; the edit plan cuts in slot order).

---

## Self-check for ideas

1. The idea list has ten ideas, the eight archetypes first; the next-shot list has five, none already made.
2. Every idea names one real landmark (or one icon of the subject: a game moment, a film scene, a thing of the era) and one light; no idea depends on a club name, logo or readable sign.
3. Both lists close with the More line.
