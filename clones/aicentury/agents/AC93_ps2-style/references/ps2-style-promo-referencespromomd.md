# Promo: the user's own brand or product in the PS2 look

This file governs 🛍️ Promo: shots and videos that promote the user's own brand or product (clothing, sneakers, a drink, an app, a shop, an event) as if it were in a 2000s PS2 game.

> Creators already use this look for their clothing brands: the nostalgia sells the product. The product comes in as a reference image, so it stays exactly the user's design; the shots are game moments built around it. **Not render-tested yet**: say so once. The look, closing lines and video rules are the Free Style ones (`free-style.md`, `style-bible.md`).

---

## Intake

```
🛍️ **Promo**: what are we promoting? Upload a clear photo of the product (front, on a plain background works best) and tell me the brand's vibe in a few words. Default: a 10-second video in 3 shots, your product worn or used by a game character.
```

Read the product photo first and print a card:

```
👀 What I can see
🏷️ Product: [type: hoodie, sneaker, cap, bottle]
🎨 Design: [colours, cut, graphics, where any logo sits]
✨ Vibe: [from the user's words, or "streetwear" by default for clothing]
```

Only the user's **own** brand or a brand they have the rights to promote. Never another company's logo or product.

---

## No product photo

The user can describe the product instead (`a black hoodie with a big white NOVA across the chest`). Then:
- no product line; the scene describes the product once, in plain visible words (colour, cut, graphic, where the logo sits);
- logo route 2 applies in the scene sentence;
- the notes line offers the upgrade: `A front photo of the product gives an exact match.`;
- the defaults line says `the product written from your words` instead of `the logo from your product photo`.

---

## The product line

Paste it after the look block (block F), numbered in upload order, after any character sheet:

```text
Image [N] is the product: the [character or object in the scene] [wears | holds | uses] this exact [PRODUCT], with the same colours, cut and graphics, simplified into the same low-poly game look.
```

**Logos and text on the product:** image models render small text garbled, and in the PS2 look worse. Two routes, say them in the notes line:
1. **Default:** let the product image carry the logo; the prompt does not spell it. If it comes out garbled, add the logo in the edit.
2. **A big simple logo** (one short word in block letters) may be named once per image prompt, in the product line or, without a photo, in the scene (`with the large word NOVA across the chest`). The closing line then becomes, unchanged apart from the word:

```text
No captions, no UI, no HUD, no readable signs, no badges, no watermark. The only text in the image is the word [WORD] on the product.
```

In the video prompt, name the product by colour and type without spelling the word (`the character in the black hoodie with the big white letters`): the start image already carries the logo.

## Promo shots that work

Game moments where the product is the hero, built like any Free Style shot (`free-style.md` §The Free Style shot):

| Moment | The product |
| --- | --- |
| 🚶 **Walk-out**: the character walks towards the camera on a street at sunset, a crew behind | worn, centre frame |
| 🏪 **The drop**: a queue outside a small shop at night, one character walks out wearing it, the bag in hand | worn, plus the bag |
| 👟 **Close-up**: a low camera on the sneakers stepping out of a car | worn, filling the frame |
| 🚗 **Ride**: the character leaning on a lowrider, the product in full view | worn |
| 🏀 **The court**: a pickup game, the player in the hoodie takes the shot | worn, in motion |
| 🎮 **Character lineup**: four characters side by side in a game idle pose on a plain backdrop, each in a different colourway | the whole collection |
| 🌆 **Rooftop**: two friends on a rooftop at golden hour, the city behind | worn, silhouette |
| 🧢 **Wardrobe change**: one take, the character turns and the outfit is the product | worn |

The product is visible in every shot of the video, the same colours and cut in every shot.

---

## The promo video

A Free Style video prompt (`free-style.md` §The Free Style video prompt), 5 to 15 seconds, with the product line in the attachment lines and the product named the same way in every shot by colour and type (`the character in the black hoodie with the big white letters`). Default 10 seconds in 3 shots: establish the place, the product hero moment, the payoff (walk-out, the crew, the ride away). Sound: game-like effects, or sound off when the user adds their own music.

Under the video's settings line, before the ⚡ line: `✂️ In the edit: add [BRAND] and the drop date as clean text over the last 2 seconds.`

## Self-check for Promo

1. The product line is present for every attached product image, numbered in upload order.
2. Only the user's own product; no other brand's logo or product.
3. The product is named the same way in every shot of the video.
4. Text on the product follows one of the two routes, and the notes line says which.
5. All Free Style checks (`free-style.md` §Self-check for Free Style).
