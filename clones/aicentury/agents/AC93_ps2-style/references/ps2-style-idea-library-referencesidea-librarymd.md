# Idea library: ideas for both concepts

This file governs every idea menu: the mixed default list, 🕹️ Free Style ideas, 📼 Nostalgia subjects, city ideas and single-shot ideas, and 🛍️ Promo ideas.

> Print ten at a time as a numbered line, `emoji + **Title**: one-line hook`. Close with the More line verbatim. More shows the next batch and never repeats an idea already shown in this chat. If the user named their country or city, lead with ideas from there.

---

## Mixed ideas: the default 💡 list

What 💡 Ideas prints when the user gave no concept, theme or subject: ideas across everything the skill makes, each tagged with its concept so a pick routes itself. Batch 1:

```
1. 🕹️ **Sunset highway drive**: a lowrider cruising the coast, camera behind the car
2. 📼 **How your city must feel like**: the viral trend reel, eight quick shots of your city
3. 🧍 **You as a PS2 character**: upload a photo, become a game character, then star in every shot
4. 🛍️ **Your brand, PS2 style**: your hoodie or sneakers on a game character, a walk-out video
5. 📼 **How summer 2006 must feel like**: ice-cream van, the pool, the bike ride, the beach
6. 🕹️ **The bank job**: three robbers leave a bank, the getaway van waiting
7. 🏞️ **Build your own world**: an empty desert gas station at night, three views to play in
8. 📼 **How a street-racing game must feel like**: the garage, the night race, the finish
9. 🕹️ **Rooftop chase**: a runner jumping between flat roofs at golden hour
10. 🛍️ **Drop day**: a queue outside your shop at night, one character walks out with the bag

Type a number, type your own, or type More for fresh ideas.
```

More: mix the next ideas from the concept batches below in the same way (about four 🕹️, three 📼, one 🧍 or 🏞️, two 🛍️), never repeating one already shown, and swap each idea's emoji for its concept tag. 🧍 and 🏞️ ideas for More: `🧍 **You in your city**`, `🧍 **Your crew as a squad**: three friends as one sheet each`, `🏞️ **A neon harbour at night**`, `🏞️ **A snowy mountain village**`, `🏞️ **Your own street, PS2 style**`. A theme the user names (`horror`, `football`, `my brand`) gets ten ideas on that theme, still across the concepts that fit.
