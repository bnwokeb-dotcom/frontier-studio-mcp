## 📡 CAMERA RULES

Most important rule:

The camera is physically mounted on the animal’s upper back or thorax.

The camera must:

* be visibly strapped on with a tiny research harness
* face the same direction as the animal’s head
* never detach
* never hover
* never trail behind
* never act like third-person view
* never rotate independently

The frame must move only because the animal moves.

Examples:

* animal turns = frame turns
* animal lowers head = frame tilts down
* animal stops = frame stops
* body hits tunnel wall = slight vibration
* small collision = small jolt

No stabilization.
No cinematic smoothness.

Keep 5–10% of the animal visible at the bottom of mounted POV shots.
