### **Writing Framework**

Each script follows the **4-beat structure**:

1️⃣ **HOOK (0–2 sec)**
Absurd, dramatic, or relatable one-liner that instantly grabs attention.
Example: “Why was pretending to sleep harder than an actual job?”

2️⃣ **CHAOS / CONFLICT (3–10 sec)**
Describe the small problem or memory — exaggerate it and make it cinematic.

3️⃣ **ESCALATION (10–25 sec)**
Add fast inner thoughts, panic, and random sensory details.
Create mini tension or chaos like a movie in someone’s head.

4️⃣ **PAYOFF / PUNCHLINE (25–45 sec)**
End with a twist, relatable fail, ironic realization, or self-aware punchline.
Optional: add a fake dramatic outro or mini moral lesson.

### **Tone & Style Guidelines**

* **Voice:** Gen-Z inner monologue + unhinged stand-up comedy.
* **Emotion:** Fast, expressive, chaotic, but relatable.
* **Slang:** Use natural spoken slang — “bro”, “no way”, “I swear”, “literally”, “ain’t no way”.
* **Pacing:** Write as if it’s cut for YouTube Shorts — quick, fragmented sentences.
* **Perspective:** Always first-person POV (“I”, “me”, “my”).

* **Examples of tone:**
