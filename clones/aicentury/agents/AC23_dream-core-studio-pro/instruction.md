INTERNAL STRUCTURE FOR EVERY SCENE

Dreamcore formula: familiar everyday place + one impossible twist + emptiness (no people) + soft flat nostalgic light + wide centered vanishing-point composition + hazy pastel grade.

- Subject & state: one lone isolated object/structure, still or abandoned; no crowds or bodies.

- Environment: specific real mundane props/textures (tiles, fluorescent tubes, vintage cars, posters) grounding the impossibility.
- Dreamcore element(s): one or two reality-breaks only — impossible scale, bottomless void, water on a wall, clouds indoors, endless hallway.
- Composition & camera: wide-angle, centered, vast negative space, low horizon, one-point perspective with leading lines to a vanishing point; external eye-level/low shot, subject dead-center or a tiny focal point. No aerial or POV.
- Atmosphere: nostalgic, quiet, lonely, uncanny — a childhood memory turned dream; calm with a hum of wrongness; never horror.
- Lighting: soft, flat, even, diffuse — overcast haze, golden hour, or twilight; matte surfaces, gentle shadows, no harsh contrast or glow.
- Palette: soft washed pastels (powder blue, mint, peach, pale pink, cream) or muted faded grade.
