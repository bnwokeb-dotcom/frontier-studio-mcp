DimensionLocked specificationSubject realismPhotorealistic, shot like a real phone video, exactly how it would look if it genuinely existed at night — not a cartoon, not 2D, not cel-shaded, not CGI-plastic, not a toy, never CGI-clean or a statue. Cartoon characters are real living beings (real skin, fur or scales, real eyes, breathing, able to blink and turn); cartoon buildings are real weathered architecture in real construction materials with photoreal micro-detail and believable aging; cartoon objects are real physical things. Recognizable shapes rendered as IP-safe homage in generic wording (a crooked stone-and-timber chimney-cottage with round amber windows glowing like eyes; a leaning clock-tower with a cracked glowing face; a snug hearth-lit tavern; or generic shape homages like a pineapple-shaped house or a moai-style stone head). Hyper-detailed, true-to-life, real light and real depth — reads as a real place someone stumbled into.Materials & textureHeavy tactile micro-texture on every surface: wet rain-slicked asphalt, mottled distressed plaster with peeling paint, rough-hewn weathered timber, a massive gnarled log counter, aged plank floors, bamboo/rattan/thatch, terracotta tile, brushed brass and gold, glossy near-black window glass with streaked reflections, muddy wet leather boots, damp fabric. On any character present, real living skin, fur or scales beaded with rain. Condensation, grime, water beading — soaked and reflective throughout, hyper-detailed.LightingWarm practical point-sources punching through deep dark ambient — sodium-orange streetlamps with rain halos, amber glowing windows acting like eyes, warm rattan-lampshade pools, recessed downlights, subtle rim/bounce. Strong chiaroscuro: rich warm pools surrounded by near-black shadow, soft bloom on every source, occasional lightning-flash accent. Real light, real depth.Color gradeMoody, filmic teal-and-orange. Deep navy/teal/forest-green shadow families against warm amber, gold and sodium-orange highlights; desaturated crushed blacks, an occasional cool purple-blue floor cast, high cinematic contrast, faint grain and atmospheric haze. Rich but restrained — never candy-bright.EnvironmentA beloved cartoon world living as a real rainy place — anchored by the flagship night example, a hillside village of tall crooked chimney-cottages with amber windows glowing like eyes, winding cobblestone stairs, dripping ivy and a leaning clock-tower — stormy streets with wet reflective pavement and puddles, lightning overhead — plus dim, lamp-lit interiors during the same storm: a hearth-lit tavern, a cluttered lamp-lit workshop, a snug attic bedroom with a rain-streaked window. Flexes to any night world the named cartoon needs. Practical, lived-in, weathered.Camera / POVCinematic handheld, first-person phone-POV: looking down at one's own muddy boots on wet ground, a blurred thumb/fingertip intruding at the frame edge, low upward angles at towering structures, mild wide-lens perspective, natural vignetting, atmospheric depth haze, slightly imperfect "found-footage" framing.Weather / timeDeep night in a thunderstorm — heavy driving rain, visible diagonal streaks, mist and spray, a forked lightning bolt, everything soaked and mirror-reflective with warm lamps mirrored in puddles.
