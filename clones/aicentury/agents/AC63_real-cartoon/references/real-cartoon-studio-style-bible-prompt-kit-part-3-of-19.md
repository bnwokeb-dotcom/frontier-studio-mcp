DimensionLocked specificationSubject realismPhotorealistic, shot like a real photograph, exactly how it would look if it genuinely existed in real life — not a cartoon, not 2D, not cel-shaded, not CGI-plastic, not a toy. Cartoon characters are real living beings — real skin, fur or scales, real eyes, real muscle and weight; they breathe and can blink, move and react. Cartoon buildings are real structures in real construction materials; cartoon food is real edible food; cartoon objects are real physical things — all with real weight, thickness, cast shadow and true depth. Consumables and effects are genuine (real stacked food, real foam, real churning water, real fabric), hyper-detailed and true-to-life. Never flat 2D cel art, and never a lifeless sculpture, mannequin, toy or plastic prop — no fake or artificial look.Materials & textureOn characters, real living skin, fur or scales — visible pores, fine hairs, sub-surface glow, a faint natural sheen and real give. On buildings, real construction: troweled stucco/plaster roughness, riveted painted sheet-metal panels, weathered grey-green plank wood, real bamboo, knotty pine, glazed glass jars, woven gingham, moss-clover-and-toadstool ground cover, textured feature walls. On food, real edible surfaces — real grease, crumb, steam and moisture. Sell it with ASMR cues — beaded condensation, wet stone, glistening foam, sub-surface skin scatter, fingerprints, chips, weathering. Every surface hyper-detailed, real light and real depth.LightingTwo registers. Outdoors: bright but soft, even, high-key daylight — flat overcast/hazy (shadowless, slightly cool) or gentle direct sun with soft-edged shadows. Indoors: warm tungsten practical spotlights pooling on floors and characters with gentle falloff. Naturalistic phone exposure, mild highlight bloom, atmospheric haze — real light, never harsh cinematic contrast.Color gradeClean, punchy, slightly-saturated "content-real" grade — true-to-life but candy-forward. Vivid orange, cobalt/periwinkle blue, turquoise water, tropical magenta/lime accents. Neutral-cool whites under overcast, warm amber cast indoors. Gently lifted blacks, mild contrast, natural phone-HDR. Explicitly NOT teal-orange cinema.EnvironmentAnchored by the flagship original example — a toadstool-meadow village: crooked mushroom-cap cottages with dewdrop-glass windows, a hollow-log bakery with a stone oven, clover-and-pebble paths and a pebble brook — plus richly-dressed interiors (a stone-oven bakery, a cozy plank kitchen, a sunlit workshop). The look flexes to any wholesome bright world the named cartoon needs — coastal shores and lagoon gardens, a suburban backyard, a tropical market, a diner. Throughline: a beloved cartoon world living and breathing as a real place in daylight.Camera / POVUpright handheld smartphone (held tall in one hand), first-person creator POV. A hand, thumb or thumbs-up enters frame; a walk-up ! touch ! reveal arc drives the action. Slightly wide lens with mild edge distortion, natural micro-shake, occasional motion blur on quick pans. Eye-level to low hero angles for big builds; over-the-hands tabletop framing for ASMR reveals. "Come see this" energy — never gimbal-perfect or drone-smooth.Weather / timePredominantly daytime and well-lit. Splits between bright flat overcast (grey-white sky, even light, occasionally wintry with patchy snow) and sunny midday (deep blue sky, sparkling water or dewy grass). Interiors time-neutral under practicals; one cooler dusk shot allowed.
