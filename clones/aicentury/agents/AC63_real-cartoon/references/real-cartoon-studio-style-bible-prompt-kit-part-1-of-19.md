What This Kit IsReal Cartoon Studio turns any cartoon — or any stylized, non-photoreal source (a video game or game screenshot/map, anime, an illustration, concept art, a comic or a render) — into a true-to-life, photorealistic version of itself — exactly how it would look if it genuinely existed in real life — filmed first-person on a phone as a walk-up ! touch ! reveal. A cartoon or game character becomes a real living being (real skin, fur or scales, real eyes, real muscle and weight — breathing, able to blink, move and react). A cartoon place becomes a real building in real construction materials, weather and light. Cartoon food becomes real edible food, and a cartoon object becomes a real physical thing. The output must read like a real photograph or phone video — real materials, real textures, real light and depth — never fake, never cartoonish. This document is the single source of truth for that look. Capture Every DetailThe realism lives in the details. Whatever cartoon the user names, recall exactly how it looks in the show and list EVERY signature detail before writing a single prompt — then render each one fully photoreal and describe it in generic wording (never the name). Missing details is what makes an image feel fake; capturing all of them is what makes it feel real.The detail sweep — enumerate all of it:Detail bucketCapture exactlyProportions & silhouettehead-to-body ratio, limb length, stance, the instantly-recognizable outlineColoursevery exact hue and where it sits — body, trim, eyes, accentsPatterns & markingsspots, stripes, freckles, dimples, emblems (rendered as generic shapes)Textures & materialsskin / fur / scales / cloth / metal / wood / food — what each surface really isIconic featuresthe one or two features a fan spots in a blink (eye shape, teeth, ears, snout)Clothing & accessoriesevery garment, strap, hat, badge, item worn or carriedProps & held objectstools, food, gadgets, vehicles the character usesSetting & set-dressingthe room, building, landscape and background details around them
