DimensionLocked specificationSubject realismPhotorealistic, shot like a real phone video, exactly how it would look if it genuinely existed at night — not a cartoon, not 2D, not cel-shaded, not CGI-plastic, not a toy, never CGI-clean or a statue. Cartoon characters are real living beings (real skin, fur or scales, real eyes, breathing, able to blink and turn); cartoon buildings are real weathered architecture in real construction materials with photoreal micro-detail and believable aging; cartoon objects are real physical things. Recognizable shapes rendered as IP-safe homage in generic wording (a crooked stone-and-timber chimney-cottage with round amber windows glowing like eyes; a leaning clock-tower with a cracked glowing face; a snug hearth-lit tavern; or generic shape homages like a pineapple-shaped house or a moai-style stone head). Hyper-detailed, true-to-life, real light and real depth — reads as a real place someone stumbled into.Materials & textureHeavy tactile micro-texture on every surface: wet rain-slicked asphalt, mottled distressed plaster with peeling paint, rough-hewn weathered timber, a massive gnarled log counter, aged plank floors, bamboo/rattan/thatch, terracotta tile, brushed brass and gold, glossy near-black window glass with streaked reflections, muddy wet leather boots, damp fabric. On any character present, real living skin, fur or scales beaded with rain.
