# Doodle World — Style Bible & Prompt Kit

Doodle World — Style Bible & Prompt KitThe single source of truth for Doodle World Studio. Every image and video prompt recreates ONE locked look: a flat 2D hand-drawn black-marker-on-white doodle character — alive, but always a drawing — cleanly composited into authentic handheld phone footage of a real, wet, hyper-photoreal outdoor micro-world, where it physically touches, stretches, squishes and gets wet in oddly-satisfying ASMR materials with real physics. The magic is the contrast: a paper-flat black-and-white doodle that can genuinely grip real slime, dent real jelly, squelch real mud and wade into real water.Read this before building. Reuse the wording verbatim so the doodle never changes. Vary only the setting, the material/action, and the shot — never the character, never the flatness, never the realism of the world.Two rules override everything, in every prompt: never name an aspect ratio or add bars (the user sets the ratio in the generator — use shot/angle/framing words only), and never bake in text, captions or subtitles (any words the doodle "says" go on a Dialogue (spoken) line, never on screen).! THE LOCKED STYLE CLAUSE — append to EVERY image prompt (verbatim)Style: a single flat 2D hand-drawn black-marker-on-pure-white doodle character cleanly composited into authentic, hyper-photoreal handheld phone footage of an intimate wet outdoor micro-scene. The doodle stays perfectly flat and camera-facing like fresh ink on an invisible upright glass pane standing inside the scene — a bold, slightly wobbly, uneven-weight black felt-tip outline that swells and thins with tiny overshoot tails and a faint dry-brush streak, a dead-flat opaque pure-white (#FFFFFF) fill with NO shading, a face of ONLY two short upright black dash eyes and no other features, thin black noodle limbs ending in tiny solid-black mitten/paw hands and little stub feet. Only the doodle is a drawing; everything else in frame is fully photographic and obeys real-world physics. Soft overcast natural daylight or dappled forest light, muted cool natural color, real moss, beaded and sheeting water droplets, glistening wet stone and wood, puddle reflections, soft green foliage bokeh, shallow 

phone-lens depth of field, macro handheld realism with subtle jitter. The flat doodle physically touches, grips, stretches, wrings, squishes and dents real oddly-satisfying ASMR materials (translucent stringy slime, clear jelly/gel, wet mud, bubbly foam, water) with believable contact, deformation, wetness, stringing, drip and jiggle. Pure diegetic ASMR realism only.Negatives: no text, no captions, no subtitles, no on-screen words, no title cards, no watermark, no logo, no signature, no UI, no emoji, no border, no letterbox bars; no color on the doodle, no colored or gradient fill, no cel-shading; no mouth, no nose, no eyebrows, no pupils, no teeth, no extra facial features; no smooth clean vector line, no anti-aliased perfect circle, no even-weight outline; no shading, no bevel, no 3D form, no drop-shadow or self-shadow on the above-water drawing, no glossy plastic or CGI sheen; the doodle is inked ONTO the scene, never a sticker, decal or paper cutout floating off the surface; no cute mascot restyle, no anime eyes, no Pixar 3D, no cartoon or colored character; no realistic five-finger hands, no extra fingers; not opaque underwater (only the submerged part becomes a translucent 3D body); the world must stay photoreal — no cartoon, illustrated, painterly, anime or CGI background, no oversaturation; no studio lighting, no clean dry indoor set, no tripod stillness, no golden-hour grade (except a brighter dappled stream scene); no music, no voiceover.Every still ends with this clause. It is what keeps the doodle reading as flat ink drawn onto a real wet scene — not a shaded 3D mascot, not a floating sticker, and never a cartoon world." THE DOODLE — character lock (identical in every shot, reuse verbatim)ONE single flat 2D hand-drawn doodle "character," composited to look as if inked with a chunky black felt-tip / whiteboard marker onto an invisible clear glass pane standing upright inside the real scene, always facing camera flat-on. OUTLINE: one bold, slightly WOBBLY pure-black ink contour of clearly uneven hand-drawn weight — the line swells and thins like a real marker (a thick stroke, never clean vector), with tiny lumps, dents and small overshoot "tails" where strokes cross at the neck/waist, and a faint dry-brush streaky texture rather than perfectly solid ink. FILL: dead-flat PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), fully opaque, absolutely NO shading, gradient, texture, bevel or highlight — a clean graphic white cutout that quietly glows against the dark wet world. FACE: exactly TWO short upright black eye-dashes (stubby, slightly tapered marker strokes, fatter in the middle, occasionally tiny dots), set close together in the upper-middle of the head — and NOTHING else: NO mouth, NO nose, NO eyebrows, NO pupils, NO blush, ever. BODY comes in two locked silhouettes; pick ONE per clip and hold it: (A) ROUND single-blob — 
one big lumpy white head-and-body-in-one circle/oval with limbs pinned straight onto it (tadpole form); (B) PEANUT / snowman-8 — a bigger upper head-lobe carrying the eyes stacked on a slightly smaller belly-lobe with a gently pinched waist, one continuous black outline wrapping the whole figure-8. The white blob dominates (~55Ð70% of the figure's height); limbs are thin afterthoughts. LIMBS: skinny bendy black NOODLE arms and legs, smooth rubbery curves with NO drawn joints, same black as the outline but a touch thinner, often crossed, bent or splayed. HANDS: tiny SOLID black rounded mitten/paw blobs — sometimes a tidy 3Ð4-lobe cloud/paw cluster, sometimes a rough splayed starburst of a few short strokes — always small and black (underwater they read as a small 3-prong spidery mitten). FEET: little rounded black stub feet or short flat dashes on the ends of the leg-noodles. SCALE: small, roughly the height of the real clear plastic tub/jar/cup that shares almost every frame (about 15Ð25 cm). It is ALIVE and emotes with BODY LANGUAGE ONLY — it leans in, bends, squats low with knees splayed, hugs objects to its chest, grips edges with both mittens and pulls, presses a foot down, reaches to poke, plants its legs to straddle a blob, recoils — never with facial expression. The drawing is a FLAT overlay pinned to the scene: no thickness, no self-shading, no cast shadow of its own, yet it physically touches and deforms the real materials it grips. ONE locked exception — the SPLIT WATERLINE: any part of the SAME character that sinks below a real water surface converts into a REAL translucent pale-grey/white subsurface-scattering squishy 3D body with rounded 3D black tube-limbs and little 3-lobe paw hands standing on the real pebble bed, while everything still above the surface stays the flat black-on-white drawing — the two states meeting and refracting exactly at the waterline, and nowhere else.Keep this doodle exactly the same every time. Same wobbly marker outline, same flat white fill, same two dash eyes, same noodle limbs and mitten hands, same small scale beside the clear container.
