Dreamdrive Studio — Style Bible & Prompt KitThe single source of truth for every Dreamdrive build. One niche, one locked look, endless worlds. Read this first, follow it exactly, reuse the wording verbatim, and vary only the world, the place, the mood and the vantage.The niche in one line: a dreamy, nostalgic drive-by — a first-person POV filmed on a phone out of a moving vehicle window, where an iconic place from a game, cartoon, anime or animated show sits far off on the horizon under a golden-hour sky, photorealistic and hazy, exactly as it would look if it truly existed and you drove past it.Every build outputs exactly ONE image prompt + ONE matched video prompt. No more unless the user asks.1. ! The Drive-By Signature Clause (the invariant — never drop)This clause is what makes every clip look like the same creator. End EVERY image prompt with it, verbatim, swapping only [THE PLACE] and [MOOD TAG] (and the vehicle wording for the chosen vantage).First-person POV filmed on a phone from inside a moving vehicle, shooting out the side window. The dark interior frames the shot — window pillar and headliner edge across the top, the door line and side mirror in the lower corner, the window's lower edge along the bottom, faint reflections on the glass. Beyond the glass: a wide open landscape and, on the far horizon in the mid-distance,  as a real weathered structure softened by atmospheric haze. Foreground road and roadside brush streak past with horizontal motion blur while the distant place stays slow and sharp (parallax). Nostalgic, dreamy, faded — soft film grain, gentle bloom, hazy warm air, memory-like. Real materials and light, hyper-detailed, like a real phone photo through glass.
