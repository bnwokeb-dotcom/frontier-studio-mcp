The Locked Style (never changes)Reuse this Style Line verbatim in every prompt:a cinematic ASMR fruit-carving montage in one continuous session — same hands, same fruit, same warm natural lighting; a whole real ripe fruit (watermelon by default) that stays INTACT, round and clearly recognizable the entire time, its natural skin pattern still visible around and between the carved areas; the design is a SHALLOW surface RELIEF following the fruit's curved surface — never wedges, slices, holes, deep cavities or a free-standing bust; colour comes ONLY from the fruit's own layers revealed by shallow carving — dark green rind = the darks/outline, the thin pale layer just beneath = the light/mid tones (most of the picture), the red core ONLY for tiny deep accents (an eye), never any paint/ink/dye; clean real adult hands holding a small wooden-handled Thai carving knife 
like a pen; shot like genuine handheld macro fruit-carving footage — real fruit texture, wet cut surfaces and juice, natural small imperfections, not glossy, not CGI, not a 3D render, not a soap/wax carving, not stylized.Fruits that work: watermelon (the flagship — green rind, pale flesh, red core = three tones), plus pineapple, melon/cantaloupe, pumpkin/squash, apple, papaya, dragonfruit, etc. Pick the fruit whose natural layers suit the subject.3. Colour = the fruit's own layers (never paint)The entire image is made by carving depth, not by adding pigment. On a watermelon:¥Intact dark-green rind = the darkest tones and outlines.¥Shallow cuts to the pale green/white flesh = the mid and light tones (skin, feathers, highlights).¥Deeper cuts to the red core = the boldest accents (an open mouth, a tongue, an eye, a flame, a background halo).Shading and form come from how deep the knife goes and from fine parallel line-work. Keep the cut surfaces wet and glistening with juice. Never paint, ink, dye, or add any colour that isn't the fruit's own flesh.4. The subject stays a relief in the fruitThe carving must always read as an intricate relief carved INTO the fruit, with the fruit clearly recognizable — not a real live animal, not a separate free-standing sculpture, not a photoreal creature. The watermelon's shape, rind pattern and curvature stay visible around and through the design. (E.g. a carved eagle is unmistakably *a watermelon carved to look like an eagle*, not a real eagle.) The carving is a shallow relief on the intact, round fruit — never cut a wedge, slice, hole or cavity, and never carve a deep free-standing bust; keep plenty of green skin visible so the fruit reads as a whole watermelon at every stage.5. Realism & Physical Cause-and-EffectIt must look like a real person physically carving a real fruit — never CGI, 3D animation, a stylized render, or disconnected AI clips. Every change has a visible cause:
¥rind is removed only where the blade cuts;¥a line or detail appears only where the knife carves it;¥a peel curls away only when the knife lifts it;¥the red core shows only where a deeper cut reaches it.Nothing self-carves, morphs, or has details/peels appear on their own. Keep a hand + knife in frame at every change — the only change between states is hand-caused. The viewer always sees how each cut is made.6. Strict Chronological Order & No Premature RevealThe carving only ever moves FORWARD — never less complete in a later shot, never fine detail before the outline exists, never a deep red-core accent before the form is carved. Every shot continues the previous shot's exact progress.Keep it visibly rough/unfinished through the first ~two-thirds — a sketched outline, roughed-out silhouette, partial forms, few details. The full recognizable design resolves only near the end, so the reveal feels earned.7. Continuity & PersistenceOne continuous session: the same hands, the same fruit and skin/rind pattern, the same scale, the same surface, the same knife, the same lighting. Carved rind peels and curls fall and accumulate on the surface until a cleanup wipe. The fruit stays the same fruit; nothing disappears, resets, or moves without a believable reason.8. Camera & SoundVary angles to stay engaging while keeping one continuous session: overhead, side, tight macro on the blade tip, three-quarter, and a final product angle. Smooth deliberate motion, hand + knife visible, no jump cuts.Sound (diegetic ASMR only): the crisp slice of the knife through rind, peels tearing, soft scraping, a light water spritz at the end. No background music, no voice-over.
9. The Subtractive Carve Arc (6Ð8 stages)Reveal the picture by removing rind, roughest to finest; adapt to the subject.1. The whole clean fruit on the surface, plus the carving knife (and a fine tip to sketch).2. Hands lightly sketch / score the outline of the subject on the rind.3. Rough-cut the background rind away to the pale flesh, establishing the silhouette.4. Carve the major forms / planes (still rough, few details).5. Carve the fine relief detail — feathers, scales, hair, wrinkles, eyes — with short precise strokes.6. Deepen key accents into the red core (an eye, a mouth, a highlight, a background halo).7. Clean up — brush/wipe away peels and juice, a light water spritz for glisten.8. Reveal — a slow rotating macro of the finished carving.Keep stages 2Ð5 clearly unfinished; never fewer than 6 stages.9b.
