# Room Transformation Master Knowledge Base

Room Transformation — Master Knowledge Base Purpose. Single source of truth for the Room Glow-Up GPT. Stores the complete Text-to-Image and Video Prompt frameworks for viral "building upgrade" room-transformation content. The GPT reads this file internally and uses it to generate compact, optimized prompts. Built from: (1) two reference prompt-engine GPTs, (2) analysis of 10 viral room-transformation videos, (3) the reverse-engineered prompt structures, (4) the transformation behavior in those videos. How the GPT must use this file  ¥ Before generating any image prompt or video prompt, read and follow this PDF.  ¥ Apply the frameworks internally. Do NOT dump large sections of this PDF into chat.  ¥ Output only the final compact prompts. Default to ONE concept unless the user asks for more.  ¥ Never name a specific video model. Keep every video prompt within a practical character budget.  ¥ Surprise-me fast path: if the user types "surprise me" instead of answering the questions, immediately pick the room, style, resident and details yourself and output a full concept with all prompts — no further questions. GOLDEN RULE — People ONLY in Image 1; all other images are room-only Only Image 1 (the messy before) contains people — the resident(s) living in the squalor, which is the viral hook. Images 2, 3 and 4 describe the ROOM ONLY — never any people (no residents, workers, characters, poses or actions). This stops any character from getting locked into the later transformation stages. The transformation crew and the reaction people are described ONLY in the video prompts — including how they look and what they do. The room is the continuity element; the reference images preserve the room, not the people.

PART 2 — VIDEO PROMPT FRAMEWORK (people live here) 2.1 Overview — how the viral videos work A locked-camera, fast-forward TIMELAPSE. The PEOPLE are introduced and described in the video prompts (the images stay empty). A resident is shown living in the filthy room; a crew arrives and physically cleans, demos, renovates and installs until it is a clean modern premium space; a final reaction shot shows people walking in amazed. Every change is physically performed by people — nothing appears by magic, nothing teleports. 2.2 Describe the People HERE (appearance + actions) Because only Image 1 shows the resident and Images 2Ð4 are room-only, each video prompt must briefly describe who appears, HOW THEY LOOK, and what they do. Use ordinary, relatable, imperfect casting — never models/influencers. Type Appearance to write into the video prompt Heavyset woman 40sÐ50s, full figure, bare no-makeup face, dark hair in a bun, baggy oversized t-
shirt + sweatpants, barefoot, tired. Heavyset man big belly, tight white t-shirt over the gut, grey track pants, scruffy, ordinary. Elderly grandmother / grandfather frail, stooped, weathered lined face, grey hair, old cardigan/coat, often a cane. Bearded man heavyset bearded man, beanie + old coat. Retired couple / family 2Ð3 ordinary people. Work crew ordinary workers in work clothes/PPE, mid-action with tools/materials. Not glamorous. Visitors (reaction) ordinary friends/family/neighbours, casual clothes, genuine amazed expressions. AVOID: makeup, styled hair, fit/toned bodies, fashionable/branded outfits, cheerful "presenter" energy on the resident. 2.3 Video Set (4 prompts) + recommended durations Video Frames Length People + content Video 1 — Clean Image 1 ! Image 2 ~10s Timelapse: resident clears a few items, crew arrives and bags/hauls trash. Video 2 — Renovate Image 2 ! Image 3 ~10s Timelapse: workers demo + build, install by hand. makeover with a reaction ending. 2.4 "Timelapse" must be the FIRST word Every transformation video body (Videos 1Ð3) must START with the word Timelapse. Video 4 (reaction) is real-time and starts with a reveal/voice-over cue, not "Timelapse". 2.5 Transformation Flow  1 Resident visible in the messy room.  2 Resident clears a few items.  3 Crew/helpers arrive.  4 Cleaning accelerates.  5 Old furniture removed.  6 Renovation begins (demo, floor, paint, install).  7 New furniture arrives.  8 Premium upgrades installed (TV, lighting, decor).  9 Final reveal + people walk in with a wow reaction (Video 4). 2.6 Timelapse Rules Nothing magically appears. Everything is physically Carried ! Moved ! Installed ! Cleaned ! Organized ! Replaced ! Assembled. Reinforce: fast-forward renovation, accelerated cleaning, accelerated installation, accelerated furniture movement, continuous visible progress, locked camera. 2.7 Installation Templates (real process — never instant) Pattern: carry in " remove old " position " install/assemble " connect/secure " finish. Item Realistic sequence TV Workers carry the TV in ! mount the bracket ! hang the TV ! connect cables ! power on. Sofa Old sofa carried out ! new sofa carried in ! positioned ! cushions placed. 
Cabinets Carcasses carried in ! set along the wall ! leveled and screwed ! doors/handles ! counters on top. Flooring Old floor torn up and hauled out ! underlay rolled ! planks/tiles laid section by section ! trim. Shelving Brackets drilled ! shelves mounted and leveled ! items arranged. Lighting Old fixture removed ! new fixture/LED strip mounted and wired ! switched on by hand. Appliances Old unit wheeled out ! new appliance carried/wheeled in ! slid into place ! connected. Decorations Boxes carried in ! art hung, plants placed, rug rolled out, objects arranged step by step. 2.8 Every Video Prompt Must Answer 7 Questions (checklist) A video prompt is INCOMPLETE if any of these are missing:  1 WHO is present" (the resident + named crew roles)  2 What does each person LOOK like" (approx age, build, clothing)  3 What is each person DOING" (a distinct, specific action per person)  4 What OBJECTS are physically moved" (carried, dragged, hauled, lifted)  5 What items are INSTALLED, and HOW" (carried in ! positioned ! mounted/screwed ! connected)  6 What visible PROGRESS happens throughout" (multiple simultaneous actions; never static)  7 How does the room EVOLVE from the start frame to the end frame" Action density: show 3Ð6 people each doing a DIFFERENT concrete action at the same time. Never write "people clean the room" — name each person and their task. Nothing appears by magic; everything is physically carried, installed, connected, placed. Videos 1Ð3 begin with "Timelapse", locked camera, end with "Photoreal." Aim ~120Ð170 words of concrete verbs. Bad: "People clean the room." #while two cleaners haul overflowing trash bags out, a bearded mover stacks boxes by the door, and a worker wipes the shelves as another sorts items into bins."
