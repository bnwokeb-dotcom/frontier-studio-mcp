# Mini Kittens — Idea Library

# Idea library

Every idea here comes from what the 16 reference reels actually do. Menus are printed ten at a time;
`More` moves to the next batch and never repeats one already shown.

> **The four-word test.** Every premise in this niche is nameable in about four words — *kittens hold
> bouquets*, *kittens wreck the paint*, *open a box of kittens*. If an idea needs a sentence to
> explain, it will not read in the first second. Cut it down or drop it.

---

## 🎭 The six archetypes — pick one, stay in it

One archetype per build. A new archetype is a new reel.

**1️⃣ 🚪 The container reveal** — the strongest performer. A mundane closed thing is opened and holds a
complete world. Delivery box, glovebox, wine cooler, gift box, oven, fridge, washing machine, takeaway
bag. Camera outside the opening looking in, the opener's own hand or shoe at the frame edge, the
container's own light as the key, the room around it falling away. Closed → opened → the world inside →
every head lifts into the lens.

**2️⃣ 🍽️ The human-food tabletop** — the diner's own seat. Kittens working at adult food on the surface
it is actually served on. Lens a hand's width above the table, a cup cropped by the near corner, one
warm practical overhead. Already mid-meal in the first frame — never a setup shot of empty food.

**3️⃣ 🔧 The job crew** — a chore performed competently. Dishwasher crew, baristas, oven cooks, repair
shop, gaming crew. Camera planted straight in front of the station, the whole apparatus filling the
frame like scaffolding, the appliance's own bulb as the key. Dirty wide → work → clean wide.

**4️⃣ 💐 The receiving line** — they came to you. A neat row on a step, a sill or a threshold, each
kitten holding one prop, every face tipped up into the lens. Composed like a row of collectibles: even
spacing, identical posture, one prop per kitten in a different colour each.

**5️⃣ 🏠 The miniature den** — a furnished room inside a mundane object. Doll-scale furniture inside a
real container, dressed down to the plant and the drink cup, kittens living in it like tenants. Shot
from near-overhead, a working miniature practical inside the set as the key.

**6️⃣ 🎪 The street stage** — found footage outdoors at night. A kerb, a porch, a pavement, a forecourt
used as a stage. Often two tiers: one performer raised, the audience row below, backs to camera.

## 🧩 Building a menu

- Print **ten**, numbered, each `emoji + **Title** — one-line hook`.
- Close with the standard line verbatim:
  `Type a number, type your own, or type More for fresh ideas.`
- `More` moves to the next batch. After batch 4, generate fresh ideas by crossing an archetype with a
  new container, appliance, food or occasion — and keep the four-word test.
- When the user types their own idea, name which archetype it lands in and build from that archetype's
  camera, light and structure.

## 💡 Suggesting the next one

Never end a build without an offer. Pick from the same archetype for a series, or jump archetypes for
variety:
`💡 Next — same crew, new job: [idea from the same archetype]. Or a different angle entirely: [idea from another archetype].`
